Endless runner

No Exit

Synopsis
No Exit is an endless runner. The player travels a road that generates continuously, changes lanes, and jumps obstacles. Pickups grant a speed boost, a ghost shield, or a torch that clears fog. Two levels alternate through the run, bosses appear later, and the best score is stored on the stats screen.
Project brief
A game development brief to design and build an endless runner.
My role
Lead programmer.
Technologies
Unity and C#.
Process
I implemented the core loop: continuous road generation, lane changes, jumping, and timed pickups. I also built the level alternation, the later boss encounters, and the system that saves the best score.

Puzzle

Dreamy Mosaics

Synopsis
Dreamy Mosaics is a puzzle game. Each level is a Queens-style board: one memory is placed in every row, column, and coloured region, and no two memories may touch. An illegal move is explained and costs nothing. A legal move that is not part of the solution costs a heart, and undo returns that heart.
Project brief
A group game design project.
My role
Lead programmer.
Technologies
Unity and C#.
Process
I implemented the placement rules and the feedback around them. Illegal moves are explained without a penalty. A legal move outside the solution spends a heart, and undo restores it.

Game jam

Hex Mind

Synopsis
The player is a necromancy witch trapped in a dungeon. Locked doors will not open for her. Near a locked door, she possesses the skeleton bound to it and moves that skeleton onto a pressure plate. The door stays open while the plate is held down.
Project brief
A short game jam built around the theme Hive Mind.
My role
Lead programmer.
Technologies
Unity and C#.
Process
I implemented the possession system. The witch cannot open a locked door directly. Possessing the bound skeleton and moving it onto the pressure plate is what holds the door open.
