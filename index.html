Endless runner

No Exit

No Exit is an endless runner built in Unity and C# for a university project. The ground keeps spawning ahead of the player. Obstacles and boss encounters break the run, and pickups — fuel, a ghost orb, and a torch — sit in the path. The project also includes a main menu, a follow camera, audio, and saving with Firebase.
